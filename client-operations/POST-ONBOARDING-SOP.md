# OneTap Creative Post-Onboarding SOP

Use this after a client has completed all three activation steps:

1. Square Contract shows **signed/completed**.
2. First recurring Square payment shows **successful**.
3. OneTap onboarding form has been submitted.

Do not begin production until all three are confirmed.

## 1. Verify the activation record

Confirm and record:

- Client/business name
- Selected plan: **Start** or **Grow**
- Monthly price: **$99 / $149**
- Agreement signed date
- First successful payment date
- Onboarding submission received
- Primary contact email and phone
- Primary website action
- Service area or public location
- Main services
- Photos/logo/content received
- Requested launch timing

If anything required for the website is missing, move the client to **Waiting on Client** instead of starting the build.

## 2. Assign the client status

Use one of these statuses in the OneTap lead/client tracker:

- **Onboarding Received** — form arrived; intake is being reviewed.
- **Waiting on Client** — required content, access, approval, or clarification is missing.
- **Ready to Build** — agreement, payment, onboarding, and required website content are complete.
- **Build in Progress** — active website production.
- **Client Review — Round 1**
- **Revision — Round 2**
- **Approved for Launch**
- **Live — Monthly Care**
- **Paused — Payment / Client**
- **Closed / Canceled**

For Grow, Google Business Profile work is tracked separately and must not block an otherwise-ready website launch.

## 3. Create the production brief

Before touching code, summarize the onboarding into a short internal brief:

- Business name
- Plan
- Primary CTA
- Top 3–5 services
- Highest-value service
- Primary city/service area
- Website style
- Preferred colors
- Proof: reviews, photos, years in business, licenses, awards, before/after work
- Customer questions to answer
- Contact destination
- Social links
- Existing booking link, if any
- Domain status
- Grow-only Google Business Profile status

The brief should make the website direction understandable in under two minutes.

## 4. Define the first-build structure

Default to one focused mobile-first page with approximately 8–10 sections.

Recommended structure:

1. Hero — clear value proposition + primary CTA
2. Trust / proof
3. Main services
4. Highest-value or priority service
5. Why choose the business
6. Work / gallery / before-and-after
7. Reviews / credibility
8. Service area or location
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA / contact

Change the order when the client’s business or primary action calls for it.

## 5. Start/Grow production split

### OneTap Start

Build only the website scope:

- Mobile-first website
- Primary conversion action
- Hosting / SSL / backups
- SEO essentials
- Contact or booking flow
- Two organized prelaunch revision rounds

Monthly update allowance after launch: **15 minutes**.

Do not create a Google Business Profile task for Start unless the client later upgrades or purchases separately approved work.

### OneTap Grow

Build everything in Start plus:

- Advanced SEO foundation
- Local keyword mapping
- Structured data/local search alignment
- Google Search Console setup/review
- Indexing review
- Google Business Profile assistance
- Basic ongoing search-health checks

Monthly update allowance after launch: **30 minutes**.

Use `GBP-FAST-TRACK.md` for the Google Business Profile workflow. Website production and launch continue even while Google ownership or verification is pending.

## 6. First-review deadline

The standard first-review target is:

**7–10 business days after all required website information and content are received.**

If the client causes a delay by withholding content, access, feedback, or approval, move the status to **Waiting on Client** and reset the active production timeline when the missing item is received.

## 7. Client communication after onboarding

If onboarding is complete and nothing is missing, send:

> Thanks — we have everything needed to begin your OneTap Creative build. Your project is now in production. Our standard target for the first website review is 7–10 business days after receiving all required content. We’ll reach out sooner if we need clarification on anything.

If something is missing, send one consolidated request rather than multiple separate messages.

Example:

> Thanks — we received your onboarding. Before production begins, we still need the following: [ITEMS]. Once those are received, we’ll move your project into production and start the 7–10 business-day first-review window.

## 8. Build completion checkpoint

Before sending Round 1, verify:

- Mobile layout
- Desktop layout
- Primary CTA works
- Forms / calls / texts / booking links work
- Business facts match onboarding
- Service area is accurate
- Photos are authorized and optimized
- No placeholder content remains
- Metadata / title / description are present
- Basic schema is correct
- HTTPS works
- No broken links
- Start/Grow scope is respected

For Grow, Google Business Profile status may still be pending.

## 9. Revision workflow

### Round 1

Send the first-review link and ask the client to collect all requested changes into one organized response.

Status: **Client Review — Round 1**

### Round 2

Apply the approved Round 1 changes and send the second/final standard revision review.

Status: **Revision — Round 2**

Anything beyond the included rounds or outside the signed scope may require additional written scope and fees.

## 10. Launch handoff

When the client approves:

1. Mark **Approved for Launch**.
2. Run the OneTap launch checklist.
3. Verify domain, HTTPS, forms, analytics, SEO essentials, and mobile behavior.
4. Launch.
5. Confirm the live URL with the client.
6. Change status to **Live — Monthly Care**.
7. Record the monthly billing date and update allowance.

For Grow, continue any pending Google Business Profile work separately.

## 11. Monthly care handoff

### Start

Track:

- Website uptime / HTTPS / domain
- Primary form or CTA
- Broken links
- Requested updates
- Minutes used out of 15
- One useful next recommendation

### Grow

Track everything in Start plus:

- Search Console
- Indexing
- Search queries / impressions / clicks
- Google Business Profile status
- Local visibility issues
- Minutes used out of 30
- One useful SEO / visibility recommendation

## Operating rule

A client should always have **one clear status, one clear next action, and one owner-side deadline**.

If you cannot answer “What happens next for this client?” in a few seconds, update the tracker before doing more production work.

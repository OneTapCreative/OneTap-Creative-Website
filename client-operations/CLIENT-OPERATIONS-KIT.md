# OneTap Creative Client Operations Kit

> Business template. Personalize for each client and obtain qualified legal review before relying on the agreement as final legal advice.

## Required client journey

Lead request → reply within one business day → discovery call → recommend **Start or Grow** → personalized scope → signed agreement → private Square recurring invoice → first credit/debit card payment → mobile onboarding → build → two revision rounds → approval → **website launch** → monthly care.

For Grow clients, Google Business Profile runs in parallel and never blocks an otherwise-ready website launch. Use `GBP-FAST-TRACK.md` for the exact workflow.

## Discovery call: sell the outcome, not the jargon

Opening:

> “I want to understand your business and what customers see when they look you up. The goal is not just to give you a website—it is to make sure your online presence reflects the quality of the work you already do. Then I can recommend whether Start or Grow makes sense.”

Ask:
1. When someone looks up your business right now, are you happy with what they see?
2. Which services produce your best or highest-value customers?
3. Roughly what is a typical good job, booking, or customer worth to the business?
4. Which city or realistic service area do you serve?
5. Should visitors book, call, text, request a quote, visit, or contact you?
6. How do customers find you today?
7. Do you already have a Google Business Profile, and if so, do you know which Google Account owns it?
8. What proof can we show: photos, experience, reviews, licenses, awards, or before/after work?
9. What questions do customers ask before contacting you?
10. What would make you feel proud and confident sending someone to the new site?
11. What would make the website successful in the first 90 days?
12. What is the preferred launch window?

Use the client’s answers to connect the service to **pride, confidence, trust, relief, and growth**. Never promise a specific number of leads or sales.

### Plan recommendation rule

Recommend **OneTap Start — $99/month** when the main need is:
- look professional online
- have a clean mobile website
- show services/work/business information
- make booking, calling, contacting, or requesting a quote easy
- have hosting, maintenance, and website support handled

Recommend **OneTap Grow — $149/month** when the client also wants:
- Google Business Profile setup/optimization assistance
- advanced SEO and local keyword mapping
- Search Console and indexing review
- schema/local search alignment
- stronger ongoing search-health support
- more monthly update time

If the client does not know how to create or manage a Google Business Profile and Grow is selected, tell them OneTap will prepare the information and guide the ownership/verification session. Do not make the client build it alone before the website can launch.

### Start standard scope

One custom mobile-first page with approximately 8–10 sections, one primary conversion action, agreed domain registration/connection and hosting while active, SSL, backups, SEO essentials, two organized prelaunch revision rounds, up to 15 minutes of reasonable monthly website updates, maintenance, and founder-led support.

### Grow standard scope

Everything in Start plus Google Business Profile assistance, advanced SEO foundation, local keyword mapping, structured data when appropriate, Search Console setup, indexing review, stronger local-search alignment, basic search-health checks, and up to 30 minutes of reasonable monthly website updates.

### Client responsibilities

Client provides accurate facts, lawful and authorized content, timely feedback, approvals, and any Google-required ownership or verification participation. Client will not send passwords, verification codes, government identification, or other sensitive credentials through public forms or ordinary email.

### Domain and website

OneTap connects and manages the agreed domain and manages the website files, hosting, SSL, backups, and technical system while the plan is active. Personalized ownership, transfer, buyout, cancellation, and offboarding terms belong in the attached scope. Accounts must be current before transfer assistance.

### Google and SEO

For Grow clients, the client owns the Google Business Profile and should remain Primary Owner. OneTap may receive Manager access as an authorized representative. OneTap can prepare profile information and guide setup, but the client completes Google-required ownership or identity verification. Google Business Profile verification or ownership delays do **not** prevent OneTap from launching an otherwise approved and technically ready website. Google controls verification, indexing, Maps placement, ranking, and profile approval. OneTap does not guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, bookings, sales, or profile approval.

### Content authorization

Client confirms all submitted text, logos, photos, videos, reviews, licenses, awards, and claims are accurate and authorized for marketing use.

### Additional work

Major redesigns, additional pages, ecommerce, custom applications, complex integrations, recurring content, backlink campaigns, paid advertising, or work beyond scope requires a separate written scope.

## GBP nonblocking workflow — Grow

For each Grow client using GBP assistance, record a separate Google Business Profile status using `GBP-FAST-TRACK.md`.

### Existing verified profile
Client invites OneTap as Manager → OneTap audits/optimizes → website launch proceeds on its own schedule.

### Existing profile with ownership conflict
Start ownership request → mark **Ownership Pending** → continue website build/launch → finish GBP after ownership is resolved.

### No profile
OneTap prepares all profile information → hold a short guided Google Setup Session with the client signed into their own Google Account → start verification → mark **Verification Pending** if Google requires additional steps → launch website when ready → finish GBP after verification.

### Ineligible profile
Do not create a profile that violates Google eligibility rules. Continue the website and eligible organic SEO work without GBP.

# OneTap Creative — Square Contracts SOP

Square Contracts is the system of record for OneTap Creative client agreements.

The website handles lead intake and onboarding. Square handles the signed agreement and recurring payment. Do not use FormSubmit or the website agreement page to collect signatures.

## Final close workflow

Lead → discovery → recommend Start or Grow → create/send matching Square Contract → client signs → verify Signed in Square → send matching private Square recurring payment link → first payment succeeds → onboarding → build → launch → monthly care.

Do not send the payment link before the Square Contract is signed.

## Create two reusable Square templates

Use Square Dashboard → Contracts → Templates → Create a template.

Start from Square's **Service agreement** template. Create two separate OneTap templates so the client never has to choose a plan inside the contract and OneTap does not depend on paid custom-field features.

## Clause library

Create the clauses below in Square and add the relevant clauses to both templates.

### 1. Service term and payment — Start
The Client selects OneTap Start at $99 per month. There is no standard setup fee. The service has an initial three-month minimum commitment totaling $297. The first monthly payment is due before onboarding and begins the three-month commitment. After the minimum term, service continues month-to-month unless canceled with at least 30 days' written notice.

### 2. Service term and payment — Grow
The Client selects OneTap Grow at $149 per month. There is no standard setup fee. The service has an initial three-month minimum commitment totaling $447. The first monthly payment is due before onboarding and begins the three-month commitment. After the minimum term, service continues month-to-month unless canceled with at least 30 days' written notice.

### 3. Start scope
OneTap Start includes one custom mobile-first website page with approximately 8–10 sections, one primary conversion action, agreed domain registration or connection and hosting while the plan is active, SSL, basic backups, SEO essentials, two organized prelaunch revision rounds, up to 15 minutes of reasonable monthly website updates, maintenance, and founder-led support.

### 4. Grow scope
OneTap Grow includes the OneTap Start website scope plus Google Business Profile setup or optimization assistance, an advanced SEO foundation, local keyword mapping, structured data when appropriate, Google Search Console setup, indexing review, stronger local-search alignment, basic ongoing search-health checks, and up to 30 minutes of reasonable monthly website updates.

### 5. Revisions and monthly updates
Both plans include two organized revision rounds before launch. Monthly update time does not roll over. Reasonable monthly updates include small edits to existing text, photos, hours, services, pricing, contact information, or promotions within the approved layout. Major redesigns, additional pages, ecommerce, custom applications, advanced integrations, recurring content production, paid advertising, backlink campaigns, or work outside the selected plan require a separate written scope and may require additional fees.

### 6. Client responsibilities
The Client will provide accurate business information, lawful and authorized content, requested account access, timely feedback, approvals, and any third-party verification participation reasonably required for the selected service. Delays in content, access, verification, feedback, approval, or payment may affect the project schedule.

### 7. Content authorization
The Client confirms that all text, logos, photos, videos, reviews, licenses, awards, trademarks, service claims, and other materials supplied to OneTap are accurate and authorized for the Client's business use and marketing.

### 8. Domain, hosting, and website management
The business domain should be registered to the Client whenever practical, with OneTap receiving the access required to manage DNS and website connections. OneTap manages the website files, hosting, SSL, backups, and technical system while the plan is active. Client-supplied names, logos, photos, written content, and other materials remain the Client's property or responsibility. OneTap retains ownership of its pre-existing and reusable tools, systems, frameworks, and components. Any transfer, export, buyout, or offboarding outside normal service will be documented separately in writing. Accounts must be current before transfer assistance.

### 9. Google and search services
For Grow clients, the Client owns the Google Business Profile and should remain Primary Owner. OneTap may receive Manager access as an authorized representative. OneTap can prepare profile information and guide setup, but the Client completes Google-required ownership or identity verification. Google Business Profile verification or ownership delays do not prevent OneTap from launching an otherwise approved and technically ready website. Google controls verification, indexing, Maps placement, ranking, suspension, and profile approval.

### 10. No guaranteed results
OneTap does not guarantee a specific search ranking, number of website visitors, leads, calls, bookings, quote requests, sales, Google Business Profile views, or other business result.

### 11. Failed payments and suspension
Monthly payments are due on the agreed billing date. A failed payment may receive a seven-day grace period. OneTap may suspend website hosting, maintenance, support, or related services after 14 days of nonpayment following written notice. Service may resume after the outstanding balance is resolved.

### 12. Cancellation and refunds
The initial three-month minimum remains in effect once activated by the first successful payment unless OneTap and the Client agree otherwise in writing. After the minimum term, cancellation requires at least 30 days' written notice. Active billing periods are not partially refunded unless OneTap agrees otherwise in writing.

### 13. Pricing continuity
The agreed monthly price is intended to remain unchanged for the Client's first 12 months of continuous service. After that period, OneTap may change future pricing with advance written notice.

### 14. Entire service agreement
This Square Contract, the selected OneTap plan, any written personalized scope, and the applicable OneTap Terms of Service describe the service relationship. If a personalized written scope directly conflicts with a general website summary, the personalized written scope controls for that project.

## Sending a contract

1. In Square Dashboard, open Contracts.
2. Create contract.
3. Choose the matching Start or Grow template.
4. Select or create the Square customer.
5. Confirm the customer's name and email.
6. Add any client-specific written scope or approved exception before sending.
7. Add OneTap's electronic signature.
8. Send through Square.
9. Do not send the payment link until Square shows the contract as signed/completed.
10. Keep the completed PDF in Square; attach it to the related transaction/project when useful.

## After signature

Send the matching private Square recurring payment link.

- Start → $99/month.
- Grow → $149/month.

After successful payment, send or redirect the client to onboarding with the matching plan selected.

## Recordkeeping

For every client, OneTap should be able to show:
- Square customer record;
- signed Square Contract;
- selected plan;
- first successful payment;
- recurring payment/subscription record;
- onboarding submission;
- written scope or approved exceptions;
- launch approval.

## Legal review

Square provides contract tools, not legal advice. Before relying on these clauses for paid clients, have a California-qualified attorney review the final templates, especially ownership/offboarding, refund, suspension, limitation-of-liability, and dispute terms.

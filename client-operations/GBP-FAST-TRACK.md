# OneTap Creative — Google Business Profile Fast-Track SOP

## Purpose

Google Business Profile setup must **not delay a website launch**.

The website and Google Business Profile are two parallel workstreams:

**Website:** content → build → review → domain → forms → advanced SEO foundation → Search Console → launch.

**Google Business Profile:** eligibility check → profile lookup/creation → owner verification → OneTap manager access → optimization → ongoing care.

A site may launch while the Google Business Profile is **Pending Verification**, **Pending Ownership**, or **Not Yet Created** as long as the website itself passes the OneTap launch checklist.

## OneTap policy

1. The client remains the business owner and should remain the Primary Owner of the Google Business Profile.
2. OneTap works as an authorized representative/Manager whenever possible.
3. Never ask a client to email or submit a Google password, verification code, government ID, or other sensitive credential through OneTap forms.
4. OneTap prepares the business information and guides the setup. The client completes any Google-required identity or verification action.
5. Google Business Profile status is tracked separately from website launch status.
6. Google verification, ranking, Maps placement, and approval are controlled by Google and are not guaranteed.

## Intake — OneTap collects everything once

The normal website onboarding should already provide most of the information needed for GBP. Confirm:

- Exact real-world business name
- Primary business category
- Secondary categories if appropriate
- Public phone number
- Website/domain
- Storefront address or truthful service area
- Business hours
- Services and descriptions
- Appointment/booking link if applicable
- Business description
- Logo
- Cover image
- Interior/exterior/work photos when applicable
- Opening date if known
- Existing Google Business Profile status
- Google account email the client intends to use for ownership

Do not ask the client to build the profile independently from scratch unless they prefer to do so.

## Route A — Client already has a verified profile

1. Confirm the listing is the correct business.
2. Client opens the profile while signed into the owner Google Account.
3. Client opens **Business Profile settings → People and access**.
4. Client invites the approved OneTap Google account as **Manager**.
5. OneTap accepts the invitation.
6. Audit and optimize name, category, phone, hours, services, website, photos, service area, and description.
7. Record the profile as **Verified / OneTap Manager Access Complete**.

The client keeps Primary Owner access.

## Route B — Profile exists but someone else owns it

1. Do not create a duplicate listing.
2. Start Google's ownership-request process for the existing listing.
3. Continue building and launching the website while ownership is being resolved.
4. Mark GBP status **Ownership Pending**.
5. Once ownership is resolved, complete optimization and manager access.

## Route C — Client has no profile

### OneTap preparation

Before the client session, OneTap prepares:

- Recommended primary category
- Exact business name
- Address/service-area recommendation
- Phone
- Website URL
- Hours
- Services
- Business description
- Logo/photos

### 10-minute Google Setup Session

The client signs into their own Google Account on their device. OneTap guides them through:

1. Add the business to Google.
2. Enter the pre-approved information.
3. Choose the verification method Google offers.
4. Start verification.

If Google verifies immediately, continue to manager access.

If Google requires video, phone, email, postcard, live video, or another method, the client completes that Google-controlled step. OneTap can explain what Google is asking for but does not collect the credential or verification material through a public form.

## Nonblocking launch rule

### Website can launch when

- Client content is approved.
- Primary CTA works.
- Domain and HTTPS work.
- Forms/booking/call/text paths work.
- Mobile and desktop checks pass.
- Metadata/schema/canonical/sitemap/robots are correct.
- Search Console setup is complete or ready for owner verification.
- Client approval is recorded.

### Website does NOT wait for

- GBP video verification.
- GBP postcard verification.
- GBP ownership request response.
- Google review of edits.
- Google Maps ranking.
- Google profile approval delays.

The client receives a live website first. GBP completion continues as a post-launch task.

## Status labels

Use one of these exact statuses in the lead/client tracker:

- Not Checked
- Existing — Verified
- Existing — Manager Access Needed
- Ownership Pending
- New Profile — Setup Session Needed
- Verification Pending
- Verified — Optimization Needed
- Verified — OneTap Manager Access Complete
- Ineligible / Do Not Create

Never use a generic status such as "GBP incomplete" because it does not identify the next action.

## Client-facing explanation

Use this wording when a client is worried about Google delaying launch:

> Your website does not have to wait for Google verification. We will launch the website as soon as the website itself is approved and ready. OneTap will prepare the Google Business Profile information and guide you through the ownership/verification step separately. If Google takes longer to verify the profile, we continue that work after your website is already live.

## Post-launch follow-up

If GBP is pending when the website launches:

- Day 0: record the exact pending status and next Google action.
- Day 3: check for verification/ownership updates.
- Day 7: follow up with the client if client action is required.
- Day 14: troubleshoot unresolved verification or ownership issues.
- Once verified: add/confirm OneTap Manager access and complete optimization.
- Monthly: confirm website URL, hours, categories, services, photos, and profile status remain accurate.

## Completion definition

GBP is complete when:

- Correct eligible business/profile is used.
- Client is Primary Owner.
- Profile is verified.
- OneTap has Manager access when ongoing management is included.
- Core business information matches the website.
- Website URL is connected.
- Services, hours, category, description, and photos are optimized.
- No duplicate or ownership conflict remains.

Website launch and GBP completion are tracked independently.

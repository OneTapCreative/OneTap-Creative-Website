# External Account Activation

## Professional email

Planned mailbox: `hello@onetapcreative.com`.

Create and verify the mailbox, configure the provider’s current SPF/DKIM/DMARC records, send tests to Gmail and Outlook, and confirm replies do not go to spam. Only after it passes should both FormSubmit routes be changed together and reactivated.

## Square recurring payment

Create **OneTap Creative Complete Online Presence Plan** at $179 monthly. The signed agreement—not the link alone—defines the three-month minimum. Disable tipping, collect client name/business/email, test the checkout, and either redirect to a personalized onboarding URL or send onboarding manually after confirming payment. Never publish the payment link on the public homepage.

## Analytics

The site records UTM data, CTA clicks, portfolio clicks, submission events, and `generate_lead`. Enable Vercel Web Analytics in the project dashboard or connect GA4, redeploy, and verify production data before paid advertising.

## Search Console and Google Business Profile

Verify the production domain property, submit `https://onetapcreative.com/sitemap.xml`, inspect the homepage, and record the indexing baseline. Create a OneTap Google Business Profile only if the business meets Google eligibility requirements. Client profiles remain client-owned with OneTap as Manager.

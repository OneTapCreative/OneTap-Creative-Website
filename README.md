# OneTap Creative — Websites for Local Service Businesses

Production-ready static website for OneTap Creative.

## Approved public plans

### OneTap Start — $99/month
- 3-month minimum (**$297** initial commitment)
- No standard setup fee
- Custom mobile-first one-page website with approximately 8–10 sections
- One primary booking, call, contact, text, or quote action
- Domain registration/connection, hosting, SSL, backups, and technical maintenance while active
- SEO essentials and indexing setup
- Two organized prelaunch revision rounds
- Up to 15 minutes of reasonable website updates per month
- Founder-led support

### OneTap Grow — $149/month
- 3-month minimum (**$447** initial commitment)
- Everything in Start
- Google Business Profile setup/optimization assistance
- Advanced SEO foundation and local keyword mapping
- Structured data/schema when appropriate
- Google Search Console and indexing review
- Up to 30 minutes of reasonable website updates per month
- Basic ongoing search-health checks

After the initial minimum, both plans continue month-to-month with **30 days’ notice** to cancel.

## Positioning

OneTap is built for owner-operated local service businesses that do valuable work but need their online presence to reflect that quality. Sales and website copy should lead with pride, confidence, trust, relief, and growth rather than technical features alone.

Primary outbound focus: contractors and local/home service businesses such as landscaping, tree service, painting, fencing, concrete, cleaning, mobile detailing, remodeling, and similar quote-driven services. OneTap may still serve appointment and event businesses when they are a fit.

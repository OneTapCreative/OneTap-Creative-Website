Common questions

Simple pricing. Clear expectations.

What is the difference between Start and Grow?+

Start is $99/month and focuses on getting your business online professionally with a custom managed website, hosting, maintenance, SEO essentials, and limited monthly updates. Grow is $149/month and adds Google Business Profile assistance, advanced SEO, Search Console, local keyword mapping, schema, and more monthly update time.

Which plan should I choose?+

Choose Start if your priority is looking professional online and giving customers an easy way to contact, book, or request a quote. Choose Grow if you also want OneTap to build a stronger Google and local-search foundation around the website. If you are unsure, select “recommend one” and we will discuss fit before payment.

How does the three-month commitment work?+

Start is three monthly payments of $99, totaling $297. Grow is three monthly payments of $149, totaling $447. After the minimum term, either plan continues month-to-month and cancellation requires 30 days’ notice according to the signed client agreement.

Can you guarantee I will rank first on Google or get a certain number of leads?+

No. We build a professional website and, on Grow, a stronger search foundation. Google controls rankings, indexing, Maps placement, and Business Profile verification. No ethical provider can guarantee a specific ranking or number of leads.

Who is OneTap Creative best for?+

OneTap is built for owner-operated local service businesses that do valuable work and want their online presence to reflect that quality—especially businesses that rely on calls, bookings, estimates, or quote requests.

What counts as a reasonable website update?+

Examples include replacing a few photos, updating hours, editing existing text, changing services or pricing, and adding a promotion within the current layout. Start includes up to 15 minutes per month and Grow includes up to 30 minutes. Unused time does not roll over.

How long does launch take?+

Most projects can reach a first review within approximately 7–10 business days after all required content and access are received. Final timing depends on approvals, integrations, domain access, and any third-party verification.

What happens after I submit the request form?+

We review your business, goals, current online presence, and plan fit. A reply is normally sent within one business day. If the project is a fit, the next steps are a written scope, client agreement, first payment, and onboarding.

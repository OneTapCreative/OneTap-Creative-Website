FAQ

The basics. Nothing hidden.

What’s the difference between Start and Grow?+

Start gives you the professional website, hosting, maintenance, and SEO essentials. Grow adds Google Business Profile assistance, advanced SEO, Search Console, local keyword mapping, schema, and more monthly update time.

Is there a setup fee?+

No standard setup fee. Both plans have a three-month minimum, then continue month-to-month with 30 days’ notice after the minimum.

How long does a website take?+

Most projects can reach a first review in about 7–10 business days after we receive the content and access we need.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or leads?+

No. We build a strong website and search foundation, but Google controls rankings and no specific number of leads or sales is guaranteed.

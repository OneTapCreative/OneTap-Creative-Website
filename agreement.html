OneTap Creative Client Service Agreement

This Client Service Agreement (“Agreement”) is between OneTap Creative (“OneTap”) and the client identified in the signature section (“Client”). It becomes effective when the Client submits electronic acceptance and OneTap confirms the agreement.

01

Selected service

The Client is selecting either OneTap Start at $99/month or OneTap Grow at $149/month. The exact selected plan submitted below becomes part of this Agreement.

02

Initial term and monthly billing

Both plans have an initial three-month minimum commitment. Start totals $297 for the first three months. Grow totals $447 for the first three months. The first monthly payment is due before onboarding and starts the three-month commitment. After the minimum term, service continues month-to-month unless canceled with at least 30 days’ written notice.

03

OneTap Start scope

Start includes one custom mobile-first website page with approximately 8–10 sections, one primary customer action, agreed domain registration or connection while active, managed hosting, SSL, basic backups, SEO essentials, two organized prelaunch revision rounds, up to 15 minutes of reasonable monthly website updates, technical maintenance, and founder-led support.

04

OneTap Grow scope

Grow includes everything in Start plus Google Business Profile setup or optimization assistance, an advanced SEO foundation, local keyword mapping, structured data when appropriate, Google Search Console setup, indexing review, stronger local-search alignment, basic ongoing search-health checks, and up to 30 minutes of reasonable monthly website updates.

05

Revisions and additional work

Both plans include two organized revision rounds before launch. Monthly update time does not roll over. Major redesigns, additional pages, ecommerce, custom applications, advanced integrations, recurring content production, paid advertising, backlink campaigns, or other work outside the selected plan requires a separate written scope and may require additional fees.

06

Client responsibilities

The Client will provide accurate business information, lawful and authorized content, requested access, timely feedback, approvals, and any third-party verification participation reasonably required for the selected service. Delays in content, access, verification, feedback, approval, or payment may affect the project schedule.

07

Domain, hosting, and website management

The business domain should be registered to the Client whenever practical, with OneTap receiving the access needed to manage DNS and website connections. OneTap manages the website files, hosting, SSL, backups, and technical system while service is active. Client-supplied names, logos, photos, written content, and other materials remain the Client’s property or responsibility. OneTap retains its pre-existing and reusable tools, systems, frameworks, and components. Any requested transfer, export, or buyout outside normal service will be documented separately in writing.

08

Google and search services

For Grow clients, the Client remains the owner of the Google Business Profile and should remain Primary Owner. OneTap may work through Manager access. Google controls verification, approval, suspension, indexing, Maps placement, and search rankings. Google Business Profile verification or ownership delays do not prevent OneTap from launching an otherwise approved and technically ready website.

09

No guaranteed results

OneTap does not guarantee a particular search ranking, number of visitors, leads, bookings, quote requests, sales, Google Business Profile views, or other business result.

10

Payment, failed payments, and suspension

Current OneTap billing uses private Square recurring payment links with credit or debit card payment. Square securely processes and stores the payment method needed for monthly recurring billing. OneTap does not collect raw card numbers or CVV through this page. Failed payments may receive a seven-day grace period. OneTap may suspend hosting, maintenance, support, or related services after 14 days of nonpayment following written notice.

11

Cancellation and refunds

The initial three-month minimum remains due once activated by the first payment, subject to any different written terms agreed by both parties. After the minimum, cancellation requires at least 30 days’ written notice. Active billing periods are not partially refunded unless OneTap agrees otherwise in writing.

12

Entire project agreement

This Agreement, the selected plan, any written personalized scope, and the applicable OneTap Terms of Service together describe the service relationship. If a personalized written scope directly conflicts with a general website summary, the personalized written scope controls for that project.

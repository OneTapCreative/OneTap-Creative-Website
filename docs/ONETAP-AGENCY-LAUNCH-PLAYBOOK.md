# OneTap Creative — Agency Launch Playbook

**Purpose:** Take OneTap Creative from a polished website concept to a public, repeatable, legally safer, sales-ready, and scalable local-business web agency.

**Core positioning:**

> OneTap Creative helps local service businesses build a professional online presence, improve their visibility on Google, and turn visitors into calls, bookings, and quote requests.

**Launch offer:**

- $149 per month
- No standard setup fee
- Three-month minimum commitment
- Month-to-month afterward with 30 days’ written notice
- One-page, mobile-first website within the approved standard scope
- Domain, hosting, SSL, backups, maintenance, reasonable updates, and support
- Google Business Profile setup or optimization assistance
- Advanced SEO foundation at launch

## Important service boundary

At $149 per month, “advanced SEO” must mean a strong launch foundation—not an unlimited monthly SEO campaign.

### Included advanced SEO foundation

- Local keyword and intent mapping
- Search-focused page structure and headings
- Unique title tag and meta description
- Canonical URL implementation
- Mobile and performance optimization
- Image compression, dimensions, and alt text
- XML sitemap and robots.txt
- Google Search Console connection and sitemap submission
- Google Analytics or approved privacy-conscious analytics
- Organization or LocalBusiness structured data when eligible
- Open Graph and social-sharing metadata
- NAP and service-area consistency
- Internal links and conversion-focused calls to action
- Indexing review and launch checks
- Basic post-launch visibility monitoring

### Not included in the standard $149 plan

- Guaranteed rankings, traffic, leads, bookings, or revenue
- Weekly blog production
- Backlink acquisition or paid placements
- Large citation-building campaigns
- Multi-location SEO programs
- Ecommerce SEO
- Ongoing competitor reporting
- Full monthly content strategy
- Advanced custom development
- Major redesigns or unlimited new pages
- Google Ads or social-media advertising

These services require an add-on or separate proposal.

# Phase 1 — Business and Legal Foundation

## Goal

Be able to accept a paying client professionally and consistently.

## Required business setup

- [ ] Confirm business entity and local registration requirements
- [ ] Obtain EIN if appropriate
- [ ] Open a dedicated business bank account
- [ ] Create bookkeeping categories for subscriptions, domains, software, contractors, and taxes
- [ ] Create a professional email such as `hello@onetapcreative.com`
- [ ] Create a dedicated support email such as `support@onetapcreative.com`
- [ ] Set up a business phone or managed business number
- [ ] Choose payment processor and recurring subscription workflow
- [ ] Set up invoices, receipts, failed-payment notices, and cancellation records

## Required documents

- [ ] Client Service Agreement
- [ ] Scope of Work attachment
- [ ] Website Content and Approval Policy
- [ ] Domain and Hosting Policy
- [ ] Google Business Profile Authorization form
- [ ] Cancellation and Transfer Policy
- [ ] Privacy Policy
- [ ] Website Terms of Service
- [ ] Photo, logo, testimonial, and portfolio-use permission
- [ ] Accessibility statement and internal accessibility checklist

## Contract protections to include

- Scope is limited to the agreed website and listed features
- Client must provide truthful business details and authorized content
- Client controls and owns the Google Business Profile
- Google verification, approval, suspension, indexing, and rankings are outside OneTap Creative’s control
- No ranking, lead, revenue, or sales guarantee
- First three monthly payments form the initial minimum commitment
- Major redesigns, additional pages, ecommerce, custom software, and advanced integrations require a quote
- A defined number of revision rounds is included
- Work pauses when required content, access, or approval is missing
- Late or failed payment procedures are stated
- Website suspension and restoration procedures are stated
- Domain transfer, website-file transfer, buyout, and offboarding rules are explicit
- OneTap Creative may display completed work only when permission is granted

## Completion standard

A client can sign, pay, receive a receipt, and understand the full service boundary before production starts.

# Phase 4 — Payment and Client Onboarding

## Goal

Collect everything required before production begins.

## Client activation order

1. Client accepts proposal
2. Client signs agreement
3. First subscription payment succeeds
4. Client receives welcome email
5. Client completes intake form
6. Client uploads content and grants access
7. OneTap Creative verifies content completeness

### Business identity

- Legal and public business name
- Primary contact
- Email and phone
- Business address or service area
- Business hours
- Primary and secondary services
- Main customer action
- Target cities and communities
- Existing website and social profiles
- Competitors or examples they like

### Website content

- Logo files
- Brand colors
- Service descriptions
- Pricing or starting prices
- About/business story
- Photos and videos
- Reviews/testimonials and permission to use them
- Booking, phone, quote, or contact destination
- Required disclaimers, licenses, or policies

### Google Business Profile

- Existing profile status
- Primary owner email
- Current business category
- Address visibility preference
- Service areas
- Verification status
- Existing suspension or duplicate-profile issues
- Permission for OneTap Creative to assist as manager

## Content-ready rule

Production does not begin until required content is received or the client approves placeholder/copywriting support.

## Completion standard

The project has signed authority, successful payment, complete content, access, and a documented scope.

# Phase 5 — Website Production SOP

## Goal

Build every client site through the same quality-controlled process.

## Step 1: Project setup

- [ ] Create client folder and project record
- [ ] Create or duplicate the correct industry template
- [ ] Create GitHub repository under the approved organization/account
- [ ] Create Vercel project
- [ ] Set internal project name and status
- [ ] Save all client-provided content and permissions

## Step 2: Strategy

- [ ] Identify primary conversion: call, book, request quote, or visit
- [ ] Define target customer
- [ ] Define primary city/service area
- [ ] Map primary keyword and supporting service phrases
- [ ] Define page structure and proof elements

## Step 3: Build

Standard one-page structure:

1. Header and primary CTA
2. Hero with value proposition
3. Trust indicators
4. Services
5. Work/gallery
6. About or differentiator
7. Reviews
8. Service area
9. Process or FAQs
10. Contact, booking, or quote form
11. Footer and policies

## Step 4: Advanced SEO foundation

- [ ] Search intent and keyword map completed
- [ ] One unique H1
- [ ] Logical H2/H3 structure
- [ ] Unique title and description
- [ ] Canonical URL
- [ ] Descriptive URLs
- [ ] Optimized images and alt text
- [ ] Open Graph metadata
- [ ] Organization/LocalBusiness schema when eligible
- [ ] Sitemap and robots files
- [ ] Internal anchor links
- [ ] NAP consistency
- [ ] Service-area language without keyword stuffing
- [ ] People-first copy that accurately describes the business

## Step 5: Conversion and quality assurance

- [ ] CTA visible above the fold
- [ ] Phone and form links work
- [ ] Form delivers successfully
- [ ] Confirmation page works
- [ ] Spam controls are active
- [ ] Required fields are clear
- [ ] Navigation works on mobile
- [ ] No text overlap or horizontal overflow
- [ ] Images are licensed or client-owned
- [ ] No false reviews, awards, claims, addresses, or credentials
- [ ] Accessibility review completed
- [ ] Performance review completed
- [ ] Broken-link review completed

## Step 6: Client review

- [ ] Send one organized review link
- [ ] Client returns one consolidated revision list
- [ ] Complete revision round one
- [ ] Complete final revision round if needed
- [ ] Obtain written launch approval

## Completion standard

The website passes design, mobile, content, accessibility, SEO, form, and legal-content checks before domain launch.

# Phase 6 — Google Business Profile SOP

## Goal

Help eligible clients create or improve a client-owned profile without creating ownership risk.

## Ownership rule

The client should be the primary owner of the Business Profile. OneTap Creative should be added as a manager or approved owner only when needed for service delivery.

## Setup or optimization checklist

- [ ] Confirm the business is eligible
- [ ] Search for existing and duplicate profiles
- [ ] Use the real-world business name
- [ ] Select the most accurate primary category
- [ ] Add accurate secondary categories only when appropriate
- [ ] Add truthful address or service-area configuration
- [ ] Add phone, website, hours, services, and description
- [ ] Add approved logo, cover, and real business photos
- [ ] Connect the final website URL
- [ ] Guide the client through verification
- [ ] Record ownership and manager access
- [ ] Document any rejection, suspension, or duplicate issue

## Prohibited shortcuts

- No fake address
- No virtual office without valid staffed eligibility
- No keyword stuffing in the business name
- No duplicate profile for the same business without eligibility
- No fake reviews
- No ownership locked only to OneTap Creative

## Completion standard

The client retains ownership, all details are truthful, access is documented, and the website/profile information matches.

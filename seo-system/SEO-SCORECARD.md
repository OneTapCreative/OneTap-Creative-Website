# OneTap SEO 100-Point Scorecard

A client site must score at least **90/100** and have **zero critical errors** before launch.

| Category | Points | Required outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| Approved business truth | 15 | NAP, hours, services, prices, claims, images, and service area are client-approved |
| Search intent and content | 20 | One primary intent, useful copy, one H1, logical headings, visible local relevance |
| Metadata and indexability | 15 | Title, description, HTTPS canonical, robots, social metadata, no production noindex |
| Structured data | 10 | Correct LocalBusiness subtype, WebSite, Service, visible FAQ only, no invented ratings |
| Crawl and discovery | 10 | robots.txt, canonical-only sitemap, valid internal links, no blocked production pages |
| Mobile, performance, and accessibility | 15 | Responsive layout, compressed images, dimensions, accessible navigation/forms, Core Web Vitals review |
| Conversion and trust | 5 | Primary action works, contact details visible, policies and expectations clear |
| Search Console and Business Profile | 5 | Property verified, sitemap submitted, profile client-owned and aligned |
| Monitoring and records | 5 | Baseline saved, monthly process scheduled, reports retained |
| **Total** | **100** | **90+ and no critical errors** |

## Critical blockers

- wrong client name, phone, city, domain, or schema
- unfinished placeholders
- production `noindex`
- robots.txt blocking the site
- missing or wrong canonical
- missing homepage from sitemap
- invalid JSON-LD
- invented review/rating schema
- unsupported claims
- broken primary form, booking, call, or text action
- incomplete client approvals

## Warning policy

Warnings do not automatically block launch, but they must be reviewed. Document why a warning is accepted, who approved it, and the date.

# Search Console and Google Business Profile SOP

## Search Console

1. Create or select the client-owned Google account.
2. Add a **Domain property** when DNS access is available.
3. Add the verification record without removing existing DNS records.
4. Confirm the preferred production domain uses HTTPS.
5. Submit the canonical sitemap URL.
6. Inspect the homepage after deployment.
7. Confirm Google can access the page and resources.
8. Request indexing only after the final site is public and approved.
9. Record verification date, sitemap status, and indexed-page baseline.
10. Add OneTap access only at the permission level needed.

## Google Business Profile

1. The client remains primary owner.
2. OneTap is added as manager.
3. Never request the client’s Google password.
4. Confirm the exact real-world business name.
5. Choose the most accurate primary category and relevant secondary categories.
6. Confirm storefront versus service-area rules.
7. Hide a residential address when customers do not visit it.
8. Match phone, website, hours, services, and service area to the website.
9. Add client-approved photos.
10. Guide the client through Google-requested verification.
11. Record approval, pending, suspended, or verification-required status.
12. Do not create duplicate profiles.

## Consistency check

The website, Search Console, and Business Profile should agree on:

- business name
- website domain
- phone
- address or service area
- hours
- primary service
- service descriptions
- public pricing when displayed

## Reporting language

Use: “The website and profile have been configured using current best practices.”

Do not use: “You will rank first,” “Google approval is guaranteed,” or “This will generate a specific number of leads.”
